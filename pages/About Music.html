EXPLORE THE ELEMENTS

Want to learn more? Click the layers below!

Discover how each musical layer builds the foundation of sound.

LAYER 01

Rhythm

The foundation of all music.

Think of rhythm as a backbone. It makes music through patterns of short and long sounds with an underlying pulse or beat. It is the baseline and fundamentally the most important part of all music.

LAYER 02

Tempo

Think of tempo as the heartbeat of music.

Tempo dictates how fast or slow a song is. Usually measured in beats per minute (BPM), it sets the energy of a song, from slow and calming pieces to fast, energetic ones.

LAYER 03

Pitch

The highness or lowness of a sound.

Pitch defines a melody. Rapid vibrations produce high pitches, used in instruments like flutes or violins, while slower vibrations create lower pitches, heard in basses or tubas.

LAYER 04

Timbre

The color and character of a sound.

Timbre is why an acoustic guitar and a grand piano can play the same note at the same volume yet still sound completely different from one another.

LAYER 05

Texture

How layers of music blend together.

Texture describes the depth of a song. A single melody is monophonic, a melody with accompaniment is homophonic, while multiple independent melodies create polyphonic texture.

LAYER 06

Dynamics

The volume variations between soft and loud.

Dynamics bring emotion to music. They range from very soft moments (pianissimo) to powerful climaxes (fortissimo), adding expression to every performance.

NOISE vs MUSIC

What Makes Sound "Music"?

Both are just air flowing through your ears, so what makes music, music?

UNSTRUCTURED

Noise

  • Irregularity: Just a clutter of noise all together.
  • How we see it: It's harsh and annoying.
  • Examples: Traffic, static, anything you find annoying.
STRUCTURED

Music

  • Patterns: Sound waves follow rhythm, pulse and structure.
  • Purpose: Every song usually has a purpose, whether that to be express emotions, or simply for fun.
  • Examples: Songs, orchestras and instruments.

But that does not mean noise can't be transformed, after all music is just noise in a particular order.
