Why?

Why We Built Music Vault

Music Vault exists because 4 kids with a passion for audio and music wanted to help boost community awareness about music, its history, its beauty, the technology and instruments that make the musical masterpieces today, and the artists that bring people together. We built this site because we want a one-stop-shop for everyone to explore, learn and rediscover why music matters, in the hope that more people become passionate about it, sharing their passion, culture and loving music together.

Why Was It Made?

It was made to solve a common problem; that being there is no place where one can get all the information on music that they need. They are often through multiple disconnected websites, all having only bits and pieces of information. Music Vault brings everything together into a website that teaches you not just from boring words but how your mind was made to learn, by playing around.

When Was It Made?

Designed and built in 2026 as an interactive Web Development project for Singapore Polytechnic. But, we have always wanted to build something from the start, we just had no knowledge.

Who Was It Meant For?

Short and Sweet: Everyone. Whether you're a casual listener wanting to discover a new genre, a student researching music history for a late-night project, or a simple fan, Music Vault is for you.

What You'll Find Here

Deep dives into genres, equipment breakdowns, artist archives, and a curated album ranking, Music Vault offers a space for you to truly and thoroughly learn and appreciate the art of sound.
