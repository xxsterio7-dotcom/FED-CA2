OUR PURPOSE

Why We Built Music Vault

Music Vault exists to help boost community awareness about music — its history, its genres, the instruments and technology behind it, and the artists and festivals that bring people together around it. We built this site as a single place to explore, learn and rediscover why music matters, in the hope that it gets more people talking about it, sharing it, and paying attention to the culture around them.

Why Was It Made?

It was created to solve a common problem: music history, facts, and technology are often scattered across disconnected websites. Music Vault brings everything together into an interactive, beautifully designed central hub where anyone can explore music culture effortlessly.

When Was It Made?

Designed and built in 2026 as an interactive Web Development project for Singapore Polytechnic.

Who Was It Meant For?

Music Vault is built for everyone — whether you are a casual listener wanting to discover new genres, a student researching music history and instruments, or a hardcore music fan looking to dive into iconic albums and concert culture.

What You'll Find Here

From deep dives into musical genres and equipment breakdowns to curated album rankings and famous artist archives, Music Vault offers a comprehensive space to learn, test your knowledge, and appreciate the art of sound.
