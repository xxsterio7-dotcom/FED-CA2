Overview

One Vault, Every Stage on Earth

Music Vault catalogues the festivals that defined generations — from a single farm in upstate New York to a purpose-built city of sound in Belgium. Every entry is preserved with its lineup history, its founding story, and its place on the map, so the culture behind the music is never lost to time.

Timeline

Milestones in Festival History

1969

Woodstock

Three days of peace and music on a dairy farm in Bethel, New York — the moment the modern music festival was born.

1985

Live Aid

Two stages, sixteen hours, one broadcast — proving a festival could move the world beyond the field it stood on.

1997

Tomorrowland's Roots

What began as a small electronic gathering in Belgium would grow into a temporary city built entirely around a stage.

2026

The Vault Goes Live

Today, every festival on this timeline — and dozens more — lives on one interactive map, open for anyone to explore.
