Life Stories

The journeys behind the music

Michael Jackson

Michael Jackson began performing as a child with the Jackson 5 before becoming a global solo superstar. His albums, music videos and performances changed pop music forever, earning him the title "King of Pop" and leaving a lasting legacy.

Tupac Shakur

Tupac Shakur grew from a young performer into one of hip-hop's most influential voices. His music combined personal experiences with powerful messages about inequality, violence and society, making his work influential long after his death.

Freddie Mercury

Freddie Mercury became famous as the frontman of Queen. His extraordinary voice, theatrical stage presence and songwriting helped create some of rock's most memorable performances, making him an icon of music history.

Taylor Swift

Taylor Swift started as a teenage country singer before evolving into a global pop superstar. Her personal songwriting and changing musical styles have helped her connect with millions of listeners around the world.
